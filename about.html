About this calculator

This tool helps you quickly estimate Roblox PLS Donate payouts and DevEx conversions. Use the three tabs on the Home page to calculate how much you receive after tax, how much someone needs to donate for you to hit a goal, and approximate USD for DevEx at the current public rate.

Built for simplicity and speed — no sign-in required, and all calculations are performed locally in your browser.

Disclaimer: Not affiliated with PLS Donate or Roblox. Rates and minimums can change — always verify official sources.

Tips

  • Use the Quick Add buttons on the After Tax tab for common donation amounts.
  • Toggle the game tax switch to compare PLS Donate vs. standard marketplace rates.
  • Enter either Robux or USD on the DevEx tab — the other field updates automatically.
